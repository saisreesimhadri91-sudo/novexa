# NIVORA
Problem Statement:
- Water supply interruptions and low pressure affect residents.
- Repeated complaints are difficult to track across localities.
- A centralized complaint analytics system is needed.
Proposed solution:
-Map complaints by locality and time.
-Identify repeated water supply problems.
-Track service tickets and resolution status.
-Protect residents' personal details.

Features:
1. Interactive Complaint Mapping – Displays water-supply complaints by locality and ward.
2. Smart Complaint Registration – Records water interruptions, low pressure and supply issues.
3. Duplicate Complaint Detection – Identifies possible duplicate reports.
4. Hotspot Identification – Highlights localities with frequent complaints.
5. Time-Based Trend Analysis – Analyzes complaints by day, week and month.
6. Service-Ticket Management – Assigns complaints to municipal staff and tracks their status.
7. Real-Time Status Tracking – Shows pending, in-progress and resolved complaints as records are updated.
8. Analytics Dashboard – Displays complaint counts, resolution rates and response times.
9. Privacy Protection – Prevents residents’ personal information from being exposed in public reports.
10. Automated Notifications – Alerts staff about high-priority or overdue complaints.
11. Data-Driven Prioritization – Helps municipal teams prioritize inspections based on complaint patterns.
12. Scalable System – Supports expansion across multiple wards and localities.
